AIWebMeasure

You are seeing requests from AIWebMeasure/1.0 because your site is on the Tranco list of popular domains. This is an academic, non-commercial measurement of how websites respond to AI crawlers.

What we fetch

  • /robots.txt once, with the AIWebMeasure user agent.
  • Your homepage only, about 16 times within ~10 seconds, each time with a different publicly documented user-agent string (browsers, search engines, AI crawlers, neutral controls), to compare the responses. Nothing else is crawled.
  • This is repeated at most a few times over several months.

We publish aggregate statistics only. No website is named in our publications.

Opt out

Add to your robots.txt:

User-agent: AIWebMeasure
Disallow: /

We check robots.txt before every visit and stop immediately if our token is disallowed. You can also ask to be removed from all future waves, or ask anything about the study, by email (address on the live page linked from our user agent). We answer within a few days and remove opted-out domains from every later measurement.

Independent research project, 2026. No data is sold or shared with AI companies.
